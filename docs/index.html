Know what your insurer will pay before you file.

ClaimSaathi reads the hospital bill, checks every line against the policy and IRDAI's rules, explains each cut in Hindi or English with the clause behind it, lists the papers still missing, and drafts the grievance when the insurer pays less than it should.

Two-minute demo of the full journey, recorded from the working prototype. Or try the app yourself: it runs in your browser with the sample claim.

The demo claim

₹10,498 the family would never have known to ask for

Laparoscopic appendectomy, 3 days, a ₹6,500 room against a ₹5,000 limit, bill ₹1,74,050. ClaimSaathi predicts the insurer will pay ₹1,26,286, and that a room within the limit would have meant ₹19,649 more. The insurer pays ₹1,15,789. ClaimSaathi reads the settlement letter: the room-rent proportion was applied to medicines and tests, which the 2024 IRDAI Master Circular does not allow. ₹10,498 recoverable, grievance drafted, escalation path to Bima Bharosa and the Ombudsman.

How it works

AI reads and talks. Rules do the money.

PartWhat it does
Vision model (gpt-5-mini)Bill and letter photos to structured lines with a strict JSON schema; unreadable photos are refused, not guessed
Rules engineEligibility and waiting periods, IRDAI non-payables, room-rent proportion on room-linked costs only, ICU and ambulance caps, sub-limits, co-pay, sum insured. Deterministic, 13 unit tests
Policy searchWording split into clauses, BM25 retrieval with Hindi and Hinglish synonyms, answers grounded in retrieved clauses
Voice and languageSpeech in and out in Hindi and English in the browser; eight more Indian languages via translation
Offline modeEverything except photo reading and extra languages works with no API key
