ClaimSaathi · Problem02
Proposed solution

One companion for the whole claim journey

01 · READ

Snap the bill

Vision AI reads every line of the itemised bill. Upload the policy PDF, or use the one already on Paytm.

02 · PREDICT

Know the payout

A rules engine applies the policy and IRDAI rules line by line. Every rupee cut cites a clause.

03 · EXPLAIN

In your language

Ask by voice in Hindi or English: "कमरे का किराया पूरा मिलेगा?" Saathi answers with your numbers.

04 · PREPARE

File it right

Missing-paper checklist, deadlines, and the claim form Part A filled in.

05 · RECOVER

Fight wrong cuts

Paste the settlement letter. Saathi finds errors and drafts the grievance up to the Ombudsman.

DEMO CLAIM

Appendectomy, 3 days, ₹1,74,050 bill. Saathi predicts ₹1,26,286 payable. The insurer pays ₹1,15,789. Saathi shows the proportionate cut was wrongly applied to medicines and tests: ₹10,498 recoverable, grievance drafted.

  • Vanilla JS, mobile-first, works offline in EN and HI
  • 13 unit tests on the money logic

Why not let the LLM do the maths?

Money must be exact and auditable. The model only reads and explains; it never decides a rupee.

Business model

Free for customers. Paid by better outcomes.

1 · DISTRIBUTION

Better cover, sold honestly

"Your plan caps room rent at ₹5,000." Saathi shows plans without the cap or co-pay. Paytm Insurance Broking earns the standard commission on each policy and renewal.

2 · GAP FINANCE

Medical EMI for the gap

The customer sees "you pay ₹47,764" before discharge. Offer a pre-approved medical EMI from Paytm's lending partners. Paytm earns its distribution fee.

3 · B2B API

Pre-check for insurers, TPAs, hospitals

Per-claim fee for a pre-submission check: line categories, missing papers, payout estimate. Fewer queries and re-submissions.
